Privacy isn't an option here.
It's how Solche was built from the start.

A private account isn't truly private if your posts are stored on someone else's server. So Solche was designed to work with no server at all.

No accountNo sign-up, no login, no email address. It never asks.
No serverEvery post and photo is stored only on your device.
No internet connectionThe app never connects to the internet, so your posts simply have no way out.
Backups in your handsInstead of a cloud, you export a file yourself and keep it wherever you choose.

This website doesn't track visitors either. No cookies, no analytics, not even external fonts.
